# Pre-flight rules
- rules.ts builds the checklist deterministically and only cites the hand-checked SOURCES catalogue; the AI adds at most 6 marked items and may cite sources only by id. Why: regulation advice must not depend on the AI or invented URLs.
- Boarding passes are decoded on the device (BarcodeDetector, else lazily loaded @zxing/library) and parsed as IATA BCBP; images never leave the browser. Airline bag rules are a hand-checked table in airlines.ts with the airline's official link. Why: no reliable free API; baggage advice must not be invented.
- Seat advice (seats.ts, SeatAdvice) is hand-checked situations on a generic 30-row cabin citing SEAT_SOURCES, no AI, no accident statistics; it renders in the Plan a trip results, not in the checklist. Why: advice must not be invented, and the seat is already bought by Before you fly.
- Quick-disembarkation advice highlights aisle seats near both front and rear doors as conditional alternatives; never infer the active door from the generic cabin or treat over-wing exits as normal disembarkation doors. Why: doors used depend on the airport and parking stand, not just the aircraft layout.
- Pet rules: pets.ts holds hand-checked per-airline policies with official links plus a destination entry rule (EU, into EU, GB, US, TR, AE, other); rules.ts emits only the airline item when the airline carries no pets, and AI items about pets are dropped (ai-dedupe.ts). Why: pet advice depends on the airline and country and must not be duplicated or invented.
- AI checklist items are filtered by ai-dedupe.ts (repeatsChecklist) on server and client, and the prompt lists the fixed items' titles. Why: the AI must add only new trip-specific items, never rephrase fixed ones.
